# TEXAS OIL RUNNER — *A Golden-Hour Oilfield Dash*
**Presented by DryAire Rentals · Beaumont, TX · 1-855-4-DRYAIR**

## Logline
A rookie roughneck sprints a sun-baked Texas oilfield road at golden hour, hopping barrels, ducking pipe racks and outrunning a longhorn stampede to deliver the shift before the whistle blows.

## The Hook
It's a storybook Texas you can play in one thumb-swipe. You get a painted refinery skyline, nodding pumpjacks, gushers and a day that rolls from gold to dusk to starry night and back to sunrise, all inside one tap-to-play link that's built to be shared, with DryAire's name on every billboard.

## Core Loop (30 seconds to learn, a whole shift to master)
1. **Run** three lanes: swipe or arrow to dodge, up to jump, down to slide.
2. **Grab** oil drops to build a combo. The coin chime climbs in pitch as the streak grows, and every 25 drops pays a bonus.
3. **Collect** 5 Texas stars to light **Lone Star Fever**: x2 score, a glowing screen and a hotter soundtrack.
4. **Survive** set pieces and push past the next zone milestone. Then do it again for a higher best.

## Features
- **4 zones, every 1,000 ft:** Ranch Road → Pumpjack Flats → Tank Farm → Refinery Row, each with a fanfare and a milestone bonus.
- **Set pieces:**
  - **Pipeline Run:** slide under a gauntlet of pipe racks.
  - **Gusher Row:** dodge oil geysers that erupt across the lanes.
  - **Longhorn Stampede:** a herd thunders down the road.
  - **Big Tex**, the cameo longhorn, ambles across the road for a +100 howdy.
- **Power-ups:**
  - **Hard Hat Boost:** a speed burst that smashes through obstacles.
  - **Dryer Shield:** eats one crash.
  - **Wrench Magnet:** pulls in drops.
  - **Boot Scoot Jump:** a super-jump that clears even DryAire dryer units.
- **Skill rewards:** close shaves, clean hops and ducks pay bonus points. Combos keep the multiplier climbing.
- **Obstacles with personality:**
  - rolling barrels and tangled hoses
  - barricades and pipe racks
  - nodding pumpjacks and towering derricks
  - DryAire dryer units
- **Juice:** squash-and-stretch, hit-stop on smashes, dust, sparks, oil spray, a flickering flare stack and queued call-out toasts.
- **Original soundtrack, "Oilfield Shuffle":** banjo roll, boom-chick bass, fiddle lead and a train beat. Fever kicks it up.
- **Day/night cycle** across the run, from golden hour to moonlight to sunrise.

## Characters
- **The Rookie Roughneck:** a yellow hard hat, an orange safety vest and more hustle than sense.
- **Big Tex:** the oilfield's unofficial mascot longhorn. Friendly, slow, and always in the road.
- **The Foreman:** shows up on the game-over screen with roughneck wisdom ("Every drop counts. So does every second shift.").

## Setting
The Gulf Coast oil patch near Beaumont at golden hour, with mesquite, barbed-wire fence, tank farms, telephone wires humming in the heat, and a glittering refinery on the horizon that lights up after dark.

## Progression
- Saved **Best score** and **Farthest distance**.
- Zone milestones, combo records and a 5-star fever meter.
- Escalating speed and denser, mixed obstacle patterns as you go deeper.

## Why It's Fun
Every second gives you a small decision (lane, hop or duck), and every few seconds something big happens: a fever, a gusher row, a stampede or a new zone. Runs last 1–3 minutes, so it's always "one more shift."
